Chapter I

To My Habibti

Dear Safae,

Before anything... I just want to thank you. Thank you for existing.

I've been learning Spanish for you because every new word makes me feel a little closer to your world.

Maybe we haven't met yet. Maybe we haven't hugged. Maybe we haven't watched the stars together.

But somehow... you still became my favorite place.

Habibti, I don't need thousands of kilometers to know that my heart chose the right person.

One day I'll finally meet you. Until then, I'll keep loving you a little more every single day.

Forever Yours,

Rayan ❤️
